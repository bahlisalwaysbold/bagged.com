Seller identity verification

Bagged uses Smile ID as an external identity-verification provider for automated seller KYC. During a verification session, Smile ID may collect the identity information, government-ID details, selfie and liveness information needed to perform the check.

Bagged receives verification results and the provider job reference needed to operate seller-trust features. Bagged does not ask sellers to paste a full NIN or BVN into the Bagged seller profile form.

Identity verification is a safety control for sellers and buyers. It is separate from paid listing Boosts and does not require a seller to purchase advertising.

Bagged stores the minimum verification metadata needed to show and enforce seller verification. For detailed information about data handled by Smile ID, review the information presented inside the Smile ID verification flow and Smile ID's current privacy documentation.
